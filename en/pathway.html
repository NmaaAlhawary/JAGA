Emerging Government Leaders Program

A six-course pathway that prepares current and aspiring public sector leaders. It starts with the foundations of government leadership, moves through strategic thinking and change management, and ends with an applied project that tackles a real challenge in the participant's own institution.

Pathway Overview

The Emerging Government Leaders Program is made up of six connected courses, designed to give participants the leadership knowledge and skills needed for modern government work.

Throughout the pathway, participants gain practical tools for leading teams, improving institutional performance and using data in decision making, with plenty of opportunities to apply what they learn to real cases from their workplace.

On completion, participants will be able to lead change initiatives in their institution and build strategic plans tied to measurable performance indicators.

Learning Outcomes

  • Apply modern leadership models in government work.
  • Build strategic plans with measurable KPIs.
  • Lead change and institutional transformation initiatives.
  • Make data-driven decisions.

Field of Practice

  • Directorate Managers
  • Heads of Departments
  • Emerging Leaders
  • Strategic Planning Coordinators
  • Performance Development Unit Staff

Technical Requirements

  • Good study environment
  • Good internet connection
  • A computer or tablet
  • A modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)

Courses in This Pathway

Management vs. leadership, leadership styles, and the leader's role in a government institution.
Strategic analysis tools, the balanced scorecard, and writing a strategic plan.
Change management models, handling resistance, and leading transformation initiatives.
Reading and analyzing data, KPI dashboards, and using evidence in decision making.
Building high-performing teams, motivation, and effective feedback.
A project tackling a real challenge in the participant's institution, presented to an evaluation panel.
